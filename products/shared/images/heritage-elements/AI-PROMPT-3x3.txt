HƯỚNG DẪN DÙNG
1. Copy toàn bộ phần PROMPT bên dưới → dán vào AI gen ảnh
   (Midjourney / DALL-E / Gemini Imagen / Grok / Seedream…)
2. Nếu công cụ hỗ trợ nền trong suốt → chọn PNG transparent.
   Nếu KHÔNG hỗ trợ → để nền TRẮNG TINH (#FFFFFF), mình sẽ tự tách nền.
3. Yêu cầu output vuông, càng lớn càng tốt (2048x2048 hoặc 3072x3072).
4. Tải ảnh về → lưu vào: products/shared/new/elements-3x3.png
5. Báo mình "cắt element" → mình tự cắt 9 ô, tách nền, convert WebP,
   đặt tên và đưa vào products/shared/images/heritage-elements/

⚠ QUAN TRỌNG khi gen:
- Các ô PHẢI cách nhau bằng khoảng trắng rộng (không dính nhau, không viền ô)

PROMPT (copy từ đây)

A 3x3 grid sheet of 9 separate traditional Vietnamese ancestral-worship
decorative ornaments, arranged in 3 rows and 3 columns with WIDE EMPTY
WHITE SPACE between every cell. Each ornament is perfectly centered and
fully isolated in its own cell, never touching or overlapping neighbours.
Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF), no borders, no frames around cells,
no text, no numbers, no watermark, no signature.

STYLE for all 9 ornaments: classical Vietnamese temple and imperial
lacquer art (nghệ thuật sơn son thếp vàng, đình chùa, nhà thờ họ).
Rendered in luxurious metallic gold with warm highlights and deep bronze
shadows — a rich gradient from pale champagne gold (#F5E6B8) through
antique gold (#D4AF37) to dark bronze (#8B6914). Crisp clean vector-like
line art with embossed gold-leaf relief. Symmetrical, formal, solemn and
dignified. No color other than gold tones. High detail, sharp edges,
suitable for cutting out as individual assets.

THE 9 ORNAMENTS, left to right, top to bottom:

Row 1
1. Top-left CORNER ornament: an L-shaped corner piece combining a curled
   auspicious cloud motif (vân mây) with a Chinese-style angular fret /
   meander key pattern (hồi văn). Fills the corner, opens toward the
   bottom-right.
2. TOP CENTER crest: two mirrored Vietnamese dragons (rồng thời Lý) facing
   each other, long serpentine bodies with flowing manes, chasing a flaming
   pearl in the middle, sitting on a base of stylized clouds. Wide
   horizontal composition.
3. Top-right CORNER ornament: exact mirror image of ornament #1, opening
   toward the bottom-left.

Row 2
4. Round MEDALLION frame: an empty circular ring frame with an ornate
   carved gold border of lotus petals and cloud scrolls. The very centre
   is EMPTY white space, ready for a family name character to be placed
   inside later. Perfectly circular and symmetrical.
5. Horizontal DIVIDER: a slim symmetrical divider — a thin double line
   that widens at the centre into a small stylized lotus blossom, with
   delicate curling cloud tendrils extending to both ends, tapering to
   fine points. Very wide and thin proportion.
6. VERTICAL border strip: a tall narrow repeating column pattern of
   interlocking angular fret / meander key motifs (hồi văn) framed by
   two thin parallel lines. Tall and thin proportion.

Row 3
7. Bottom-left CORNER ornament: mirror of ornament #1 flipped vertically,
   opening toward the top-right.
8. BOTTOM CENTER ornament: a single stylized sacred lotus flower in full
   bloom, viewed from the front, symmetrical, with layered petals and two
   lotus leaves curving outward on each side, resting on gentle stylized
   water ripples.
9. Bottom-right CORNER ornament: mirror of ornament #7, opening toward
   the top-left.

Overall: an isolated asset sheet for a luxury Vietnamese clan ancestral
ceremony invitation card. Elegant, reverent, museum-quality gold leaf
craftsmanship. Flat lighting, no drop shadows on the white background.
